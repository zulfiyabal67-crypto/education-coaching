About the Project

Education begins with people—with their thinking, awareness, and willingness to grow.

EDUCATION & COACHING is the educational branch of Zulfiya Islamova (Balgimbayeva)’s professional work, connecting contemporary approaches to personal and professional development with the practical needs of the education sector.

We see development as a continuous process—from understanding personal goals and strengths to strengthening professional competencies, leadership, and the creation of a supportive educational environment.

The project welcomes the exchange of experience, professional dialogue, and international collaboration.

Original Approach

Self-Coaching

Change begins with a clear understanding of yourself and your situation.

Self-coaching is a way to explore professional and personal questions independently, notice habitual responses, identify available resources, and choose realistic next steps. Rather than seeking ready-made answers from others, it helps people work thoughtfully with their own experience.

Who it is for: educators, teachers, leaders, and anyone interested in personal and professional development.

The Anti-Goal Method

This original approach to mindful change begins with the question: “What do I no longer want?” It helps identify what no longer works in familiar ways of acting and clarify what can be changed.

1. Notice

Recognise what you no longer want to continue or maintain.

2. Clarify

Identify what is within your influence and which resources are available.

3. Take a Step

Choose one realistic action to begin changing the situation with care.

Area of Work

Educational Leadership

A developing educational environment begins with dialogue, trust, and a willingness to learn together.

Educational leadership is an approach to developing people and teams that values thoughtful decisions, constructive communication, and shared responsibility. Coaching tools help clarify tasks, hear different perspectives, and find solutions by drawing on the team’s resources.

Who it is for: leaders of educational organisations, management teams, educators, and specialists involved in educational projects.

Key Focus Areas

Mindful Leadership

Clarifying values, priorities, and responsibilities in a leadership role.

Communication and Dialogue

Active listening, questioning, feedback, and discussing challenging situations.

Teamwork

Identifying strengths, resources, and opportunities for working together.

Decisions and Development

Moving from discussing a problem to agreed actions and realistic next steps.

Area of Work

Teacher Development

An educator’s professional development begins with attention to their own experience, resources, and goals.

Today’s educational environment requires more than subject knowledge. It also calls for the ability to reflect on professional situations, adapt to change, build relationships, and identify areas for growth. This area helps educators explore their experience and find practical solutions to professional challenges.

Who it is for: teachers, lecturers, early-years educators, methodologists, and other education professionals.

Key Areas of Development

Professional Reflection

Reviewing experience, challenging situations, and personal ways of responding.

Self-Coaching at Work

Tools for clarifying tasks, identifying resources, and planning next steps.

Emotional Self-Regulation

Responding to emotions mindfully and maintaining professional boundaries.

Professional Resilience

Individual ways to sustain effectiveness and adapt to change.

Educational Programme

Self-Coaching for Educators

Professional development begins with understanding yourself, reflecting on your experience, and finding solutions independently.

This programme develops professional reflection, self-regulation, and mindful management of one’s own development. It combines theory and practical tools that help educators analyse professional situations, identify available resources, and plan next steps.
