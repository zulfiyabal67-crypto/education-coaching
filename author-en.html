About the Project Author

Zulfiya Islamova (Balgimbayeva)

Founder and Director of EDUCATION & COACHING

Doctor of Psychological Sciences, researcher, lecturer in pedagogy and psychology, and coach.

Author of approximately 150 scientific publications and four monographs. I bring together research, teaching experience, and coaching approaches to support human development and improve education.

A key focus of my work is self-coaching and the professional development of educators.
